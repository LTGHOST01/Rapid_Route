RAPIDROUTE JUDGE DECK (12 slides)
File: docs/RapidRoute_Judge_Deck.pptx
Open in PowerPoint or Google Slides. Speaker notes are on every slide
(View > Notes).


1. THREE-TO-FOUR MINUTE TALK
----------------------------
0:00  Slide 1   Pitch. Pause after: Google shows the roads. RapidRoute
                decides the dispatch.
0:25  Slide 2   Problem. Unit, route, changing conditions, explainability.
                No invented death stats.
0:50  Slide 4   "Isn't this Google Maps?" Two columns. Atlas vs officer.
1:20  Slide 6   Scoring. Four factors. Lowest penalty. Blocked = removed.
                Critical weights time at 0.55. 10 min vs 11 min example.
1:55  Slide 7   Block a road, decision changes. Leave this up for the demo.
2:20  LIVE APP  Dispatcher: Mumbai demo, assign, calculate, start.
                Admin: Evaluator, Block current route, Run all 4.
3:20  Slide 10  Trust. Demo simulation labelled. Not municipal feeds.
                Not a trained model.
3:40  Slide 12  Close. Google finds the roads. RapidRoute decides how
                emergency vehicles use them. Stop. Offer questions.

If you only have 2 minutes: 1, 4, 6, 7, live demo, 12.


2. SLIDES TO KEEP UP DURING THE LIVE DEMO
-----------------------------------------
Slide 5  while you click through create / assign / calculate

Do not flip slides while the map is the point. One idea on the projector.


3. THREE POINTS JUDGES SHOULD REMEMBER
--------------------------------------
1. Google supplies map, geometry, ETA, and alternatives.
   RapidRoute assigns the unit, scores the options, and logs why.

2. A blocked road is removed before scoring. It is never a trade-off.
   If nothing remains: "No suitable route available."

3. Scoring is a published weighted formula, not a trained AI model.
   Admin blockage is labelled DEMO SIMULATION, not a city live feed.
